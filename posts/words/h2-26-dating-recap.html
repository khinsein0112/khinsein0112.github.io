h2 '26 dating recap

As June comes to an end, I wanted to informally recap my whirlwind year back in the states as I restarted my dating journey! So far this year, I've been on six first dates. Two of them did not result in second dates. I met one of them at a restaurant where I made the first move, another one on a boat in Miami through mutual friends, a blind date set up by a friend, and three from Raya.

A Lebanese human rights attorney, a defense tech co-founder, CEO of some company I don't quite understand, exited founder/unemployed beach bum, museum art curator, and a German saas unicorn founder.

The attorney and the defense tech guy I did not vibe with upon meeting. Half of them were single dads, the other half were not. The ages ranged from 35 to 45. The CEO I put on ice because I don't think I'm ready to commit. The chemistry was questionable. I fumbled the beach bum because he wanted to see me and was actually very accommodating but I didn't have time a few months back to really spend quality time with him. I'd tell him in passing that I wish I could be shopping in Paris, and he'd ask me what dates I wanted to go and that he'd be working on EST zone but would still be there. I'd tell him I wanted to see his face and he'd ask me if I wanted him to send me a private jet that night. He wanted me to join him to meet the Dodgers on a work trip. He wanted to put me in a separate hotel room because I wasn't ready to have sex yet. But I kept having things to do like seeing friends, family, my kid, work, school, etc and eventually he got tired of me putting everything above him. He has a five year old and I have a four year old and I idealized this commonality but just poor timing.

The museum art curator and I just fizzled out after a few dates because we didn't have anything in common and he didn't understand my sense of humor. I'm very low brow when it comes to art. I met the beach bum at the heels of meeting the German. Timing has been god awful this year for me. I met the best guys at the worst of times.

The German asked me to move in with him in Munich. Which, a year ago, I would've been able to, but with what custody agreement and my restaurant opening, I physically can't. I think four were into me more than I was into them. The inverse applies for the beach bum. He was more into me than I was into him, but then the dynamic flipped once he stopped putting up with my shit and I found his masculinity quite attractive. And the German and I both fell for each other equally hard.

I'm going to see him in Europe next month and hopefully it'll answer some questions for the both of us. He's like the full package–intelligent, funny, bilingual, tall, athletic, ambitious, and he treats me so well. The type of guy I'd happily introduce to my friends and family and they'd approve. But I'm scared of getting hurt again. By another German no less. I just feel like he deserves better because I have no valid reason for making him wait for intimacy other than a shoddy excuse along the lines of "I don't want to sleep with you for you to like me, I want to sleep with you because you like me" But the punches don't really land. Not when he's asked me to move in. I reckon that's pretty serious. Again–I'm terrified of where this will lead; I'm not abandoning work for a guy a THIRD time!!

I'm still clutching onto my celibacy like my Chanel mini at 1Oak in 2018. It's the only thing keeping me still sane. But I almost broke my sobriety yesterday when a new friend at a party put a 4.5L laughing gas tank in my hands. I thought about half a dozen times of just taking one big hit. But I didn't. I was incredibly proud of myself for that but it made me feel guilty that I even allowed myself to get that far in thought. Anyway, six months! 2026 has flown by. I'm excited to see where I land in this second half.

I want to take a plunge and fall in love. I need to stop letting fear handicap me.
