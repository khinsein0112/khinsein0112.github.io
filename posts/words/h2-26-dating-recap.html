h1 '26 dating recap

As June comes to an end, I wanted to informally recap my whirlwind year back in the states as I restarted my dating journey! So far this year, I've been on six first dates. Two of them did not result in second dates. I met one of them at a restaurant where I made the first move, another one on a boat in Miami through mutual friends, a blind date set up by a friend, and three from a dating app.

A Lebanese human rights attorney, a defense tech co-founder, CEO of some company I shall not name, semi-retired exited beach bum, museum art curator, and a German saas unicorn founder.

The attorney and the defense tech guy I did not vibe with upon meeting. I was mansplained half to death off rip and there was no coming back from that. We went to the same school so I tried to fall back on a possibility of overlapping affinities during our college days but there were none. The defense tech guy was still married on paper and said the only reason he's separated from his wife and his offspring of FOUR months is because she legally cannot live in the US. I'm not even going to further elaborate why there was no second date there.

Half of them were single dads, the other half were not. The ages ranged from 35 to 45. The CEO is really chill and there's a good thing going there but I don't think he's the one because he doesn't drive me crazy. I want a man who makes me a little bit irrational. The chemistry is moderate.

The museum art curator and I just fizzled out after a few dates because we didn't have anything in common and he didn't understand my sense of humor. I'm very low brow when it comes to art. He was also majorly into sex and was always coming on too strong and not respecting my boundaries. Also I'm pretty sure he's somewhat gay. This one had to go.

As for the beach bum, I played too hard to get both by accident and on purpose. He wanted to see me and was actually extremely accommodating but I didn't have time over spring to really spend quality time with him. I'd tell him in passing that I wish I could be shopping in Paris, and he'd ask me what dates I wanted to go and that he'd be on calls half the time but he'd be there with me. Or I'd tell him I wanted to see his face and he'd ask me if I wanted him to send me a private jet that night to Miami. Invites to Masters and F1 followed — wanted me to join him to meet the Dodgers and Lakers on a work trip. Wanted to put me in a separate hotel room because I wasn't ready to have sex yet. Very understanding unlike some guys. I liked that we both came from the same state and could trade inside jokes. But he didn't drink or do drugs, and strangely, I felt like he therefore could not understand all facets of me. I can tell he's not as socially outgoing as I am, but he'd do it if I asked, it just wasn't his thing. I found him very physically attractive but his inability to use proper grammar or full sentences really grew to irritate me beyond belief. Anyway, I kept having things to do like seeing friends, family, my kid, work, school, etc and eventually he got tired of me putting everything above spending time with him. He has a five year old and I have a four year old and some part of me idealized this commonality, but just poor timing. I met the beach bum at the heels of meeting the German and I wasn't over the German yet. Timing has been god awful this year for me. I met the best guys at the worst of times.

The German I met at a New York City restaurant in the cutest of ways. Within two months he asked me to move in with him in Munich. Which, a year ago, I would've been able to and probably would've, but with what custody agreement and my restaurant opening, I physically can't now. And if I move to SF, there's the end of that. I'm going to see him in Europe next month and hopefully it'll bring some clarity. He's the full package–intelligent, funny, bilingual, tall, athletic, ambitious, educated, similar political leanings, and he treats me so well. A gentleman in the smallest ways; I've never had to open my own car door or carry my own bags. The type of guy I'd happily introduce to my friends and family and they'd approve. But I'm scared of getting hurt again. By another German no less. I just feel like he deserves better because I have no valid reason for making him wait for intimacy other than a shoddy excuse along the lines of "I don't want to sleep with you for you to like me, I want to sleep with you because you like me." But the punches don't really land. Not when he'd asked me to move in. I reckon that's pretty serious. Again–I'm terrified of where this will lead; I'm not abandoning work for a guy a THIRD time!! Besides, what would I even do in Munich?

I think four were into me more than I was into them. The inverse applies for the beach bum. He was more into me than I was into him, but then the dynamic flipped once he pulled back and I found his masculinity quite attractive. And the German and I both fell for each other equally hard. So yeah, H1 has been interesting but I'm also kind of over it. There are so many fish in the sea that I'm not really interested in forcing something that isn't working.

I'm still clutching onto my celibacy like my Chanel mini at 1Oak in 2018. It's the only thing keeping me still sane. But I almost broke my sobriety yesterday when a new friend at a party put a 4.5L laughing gas tank in my hands. I thought about half a dozen times of just taking one big hit. But I didn't. I was incredibly proud of myself for that but it made me feel guilty that I even allowed myself to get that far in thought. Anyway, six months! 2026 has flown by. I'm excited to see where I land in this second half.

I want to take a plunge and fall in love. I need to stop letting fear handicap me.
