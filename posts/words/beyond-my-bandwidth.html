beyond my bandwidth

I went through a really painful breakup three summers ago and I thought I'd be over it by now. And honestly? I think I was starting to be. So naturally, because the universe has a sick sense of humor, he comes back into my life out of the blue.

I saw him for the first time in years and I just cried. Not cute crying. Not a single tear rolling down my cheek like a movie. I'm talking three hours of sobbing while running my fingers through his hair and drinking almost an entire bottle of Merlot by myself. He was able to get a glass in. Very classy. Very stable.

I finally got the closure I needed. All the nostalgia, the pain, the suffering, the laughs...I tried to forget him, I tried to replace him, and I tried to get him back. Nothing worked.

And finally, last night, after sitting across from him and looking into his eyes, I realized that I just wanted to be picked. To be loved. To be the chosen one. It wasn't really him that I loved. It was the idea of being the girl he picked when all these other girls vied for his attention. For a while, I was. And let me tell you, it made me drunk with power. But the fact of the matter is, he isn't even fully interested in any of us. He said he wanted us to be friends again. He kissed me on my forehead and apologized for hurting me. Without either of us saying it, we both knew deep down where his allegiance lied.

To get closure on this years-long pitiful situation of mine, I had to abandon a hot pursuit of this guy who was really growing on me these past few weeks. Maybe a little too much. Which of course terrified me half to death, but I also lacked the self-control to not talk to him every five minutes. He's married and I have this strong gut feeling that he's not separated like he says he is and I don't ever aspire to be a homewrecker/mistress so it was never going to work anyway. It's always the married ones that are so cute and attentive and perfect. Well, duh. No wonder they're off the market. Anyway I think it was for the best that it ended before it could begin. I don't think I can take another heartbreak.

I cried again all day today. Tears rolling down my face in litigation class while my professor lectured about motion practice. Nothing says "I'm a well-adjusted adult pursuing a legal career" like quietly falling apart in the third row over a man. I'm a fucking mess today.

And weirdly, all I want to do is pick up the phone and tell him that I'm headed to the airport and I'll see him in two hours. But he probably has me blocked by now. I know I would. God. I don't even want to think about what I said to him last night while drunk. Whatever it was, I'm sure it was proportionate and measured. (It was not.)

Maybe in another lifetime.
