you being gay really fucked me up

I find myself so much happier without social media because the people who get the most engagement are some of the most vapid and shallow people I’ve ever come across. I think the worst gets rewarded and there’s something so pungent about that.

Also maybe I’m happier just thinking that my personal life is unique and special. Sure I’m religiously partaking in an event attended by thousands of other people but I come to my own decision on what outfits to wear and where to go for afters, rather than trying to follow the blueprint that’s “in.” I’m not doing it for a photo-op. And I hate being around people like that. But all my friends are baddies so it’s really that inescapable. I just checked Find My for my girls that I want to see this summer. One is in St Tropez, New York, Austin, Lake Como, Tokyo. Nice. We’re never making it out of the group chat.

It exhausts me to think that I have to go back on socials to promote my new business because I really wish I wouldn’t have to. I keep telling myself it’s only temporary–soon I’ll be out of that city and back to my books, pottery, and baking. I’m hoping that’ll bring me back to my peace. As much as I love Europe, it’s been very performatively lately with all the Americans crowding every street corner. I can’t wait to see my son next week though. And the water.

I still think about you all the time. I miss you all the time. I hate that I still love you and I hate that you can never love back the same way because I’m not a man. I can’t even bring myself to delete your photos from my Instagram so I just keep deactivating my account. I’m left unable to move forward. I’m left unable to open up emotionally or physically with anyone else in three years. You’re 6’4 but I still managed to date taller. I managed to date hotter. I managed to date richer. I managed to date smarter. Never found anyone funnier than you though. I miss you. Berlin next week is going to be really hard because everything is going to remind me of you again. But what can I do? All I can do is eat, sleep, drink, work, shop, read, laugh with friends and spend my time with lovers that I will never love.
