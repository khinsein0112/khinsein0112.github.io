i'm a mean girl

I'd identify myself as a girl's girl, maybe 95% of the time. The other 5% is some esoteric combination of jealousy and revenge, reserved exclusively for the girls who have wronged me unnecessarily. I don't draw first blood. I want that on record.

So it is with a twinge of confusion and guilt that I say this. It feels more acceptable to verbalize now, in 2026, because the body positivity movement has effectively reversed itself in the age of GLP-1s. Regardless, this is my space, and I can say what I want.

I sit a row behind this girl in class. She's maybe mid-thirties, and she weighs... I don't know. I have trouble assigning numbers to someone's body, partly because 250 and 300 feel marginally the same to me in practice, and partly because it feels rude. All I can tell you is she's roughly the size of Rebel Wilson before Rebel Wilson lost the weight. Maybe a little bigger, yeah.

She complains about her weight. She also rolls into class late, never without a pastry and the most indulgent iced drink on the menu from the coffee shop across the street. It's always some enormous cup with caramel and chocolate drizzle lacquering the outside, whipped cream threatening structural collapse, or it's something pink and green with syrup swirls and a name like Cotton Candy Iced Chocolate Cloud Latte or some other asinine thing. Never a simple cold brew. She downs it within the hour. Then we break for lunch, and she never fails to come back with Chick-fil-A or a steak and cheese sub, or, on a good day, a Chipotle bowl. I've watched this cycle repeat itself enough times that I've started arriving early just to see what she walks in with, which says something about her choices and, apparently, also mine.

I just don't understand it. Why are people incapable of making small swaps? Why do they then complain about how big they are? It's all within their control, and the lack of accountability infuriates me. And honestly, it's an eyesore.

I'm high-agency. If I'm discontent with something, I don't sit with it. I find ways to fix it, immediately. The idea that some people would rather complain and stuff their face strikes me as genuinely disgusting. Maybe I'm fatphobic. But I think we should all be, a little. It's not normal and it's not healthy, and in any other country, this kind of mindless consumption would be quietly, firmly frowned upon. I say this as someone who has eaten a gas station sushi roll at 11pm and still logged it.
